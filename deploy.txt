Solvn — static build

1. Upload the CONTENTS of this folder into htdocs (index.html must end up at
   htdocs/index.html, not htdocs/dist/index.html).
2. That is the whole install. There is no server code, no database and nothing
   to build — any plain web host will do.

What this copy does without a backend:
  - Search, browse, details pages, the player, watch history, favourites,
    settings and all 12 languages work as normal.
  - Accounts, profiles, playlists, comments, friends, messages, card trades,
    duels and the ranked board, the daily wheel, watch rooms and the notice
    bell all keep their work in this browser's localStorage (lib/device.js),
    with the same routes and shapes the Node server answers (server.js).
  - Two limits follow from that: an account, a card, a message or a duel lives
    on the browser that made it, so two people meet only when they sign in on
    the same one. The bell is the exception — it asks AniList for air dates
    directly and compares them against this device's saved list.
  - A fresh browser starts empty: no accounts, no cards, no staff. There is no
    seeded admin. A Supabase project added in Settings owns sign-in, and the
    social pages carry on locally beside it.

After uploading, open the site once and press Ctrl+F5 so the old copies held in
your browser are replaced by these files.
